I just built Sanjeevani for the Catalyst hackathon (PromptWars x Catalyst, with Google for Developers and Hack2Skill)! 🌿

Problem: Elderly Care & Medication Management. Reminder apps help, but they treat the elder as a patient instead of the decision-maker.

What I built: a shared care circle with secure login where the elder, caregiver and family each have their own role. Caregivers can suggest medicine or appointment changes, and the elder approves in one tap. Email reminders go out before a dose, and a missed dose automatically alerts the caregiver and family. Gemini explains medicines in plain language, and there is a daily "I am OK" check-in plus an emergency button for the family.

Tech: Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, Realtime), Vercel serverless functions, Resend, Gemini API.

What makes it different: the elder stays at the centre of every healthcare decision, with an accessibility-first interface (large text mode, a hyperlegible font, high contrast).
